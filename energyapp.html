Energy self-service App

A self-service app built for thousands of utility customers. Seven months, five real users, ten hypotheses tested. The business wanted the inbox in the bottom navigation – research said otherwise. Here's how the data changed the architecture.

The Design Process

The Energy App gives thousands of SWM customers control over their energy and water usage. During usability testing, one finding stood out: the business wanted the inbox in the bottom navigation – but research showed it belonged under "More", and consumption visualisation deserved its own tab. That single decision shaped the final information architecture.

Project Highlights

  • Architecture Decision
    Inbox sufficient under "More" – consumption visualisation promoted to its own primary tab.
  • Tooltip Pattern
    Explanatory text felt overwhelming in testing – restructured and hidden behind info tooltips.
  • Task Completion
    4/5 users completed the meter-reading workflow without assistance.
  • Consumption Visualisation
    Ranked 3rd of 10 features for both importance and usage frequency.
  • Accessibility (WCAG AA)
    Scalable typography, high-contrast colours, inclusive interaction patterns.

Conclusion

The Energy App delivers a user-centric, accessible platform for energy and water management. Key features—interactive consumption charts, meter-reading history, and tariff controls—offer tangible user value. The most important lesson from this project: data beats assumptions. When research contradicted the brief on navigation structure, the numbers made the argument. The final architecture reflects what users actually needed – not what was initially planned.
